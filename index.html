This free checker tells you if your e-Invoice is shaped right. It can't tell you the one thing that matters: will LHDN actually accept it? The paid tier runs your document through LHDN's real engine and turns the cryptic rejection codes into a plain-English fix.

  • Get the fix, not just the error — the exact field to change to turn a red “Invalid” into a green UUID.

The “MyInvois service” indicator sends a lightweight reachability ping to LHDN’s public API hosts from your browser (no document data is sent). It reports whether the host answers your browser, not LHDN’s internal health — a restrictive local network may show “No response” even when MyInvois is up.
